Tensor product modules

Apr 23, 20261 min read

Let RRR be a ring and M,NM,NM,N be R-modules. We can turn the tensor product into an RRR-module by defining the action:

R↦End(M⊗RN)r⋅(x⊗y)≔(r⋅x)⊗yR\mapsto \mathrm{End} (M\otimes _R N)\qquad r\cdot(x\otimes y)\coloneqq (r\cdot x)\otimes yR↦End(M⊗R​N)r⋅(x⊗y):=(r⋅x)⊗y
